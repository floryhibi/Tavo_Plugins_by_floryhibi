# 🧩 TavoAi Plugins by @floryhibi

## ✦ Persona Atelier

Ever opened a blank character card and just… froze? Persona Atelier lets you tap your way to a {{char}} or your own {{user}} persona — pick, don't type.

- **🪡 Patterns** — 13 character and 8 persona archetypes fill the whole form in one tap (Ice for everyone but her, Vampire collector, Card sharp, Relic hunter, Embassy interpreter…), then tailor anything.
- **💊 Pill questionnaire with emoji** — 10 short steps: identity, appearance, personality, life & hobbies, past, motives, relationships, voice and scenario. "Other" is always there for your own words.
- **🔞 Intimacy 18+ (optional)** — orientation, role, style, kinks & fetishes, turn-ons and hard limits. Off by default; nothing from it is used until you turn it on.
- **📌 Moodboard** — photos or links (Pinterest works), a note on each picture and an automatic color palette; the AI writes the look and vibe from your palette, notes and mood words.
- **🏷️ AI profile editor** — a full, consistent profile; regenerate or refine one section, consistency check, suggestions. Your own choices are marked and never silently rewritten.
- **📤 Export** — create the character or persona right in Tavo, or export Character Card V3, JSON, Markdown or text. Works with your current Tavo model or any OpenAI-compatible API.

## 🎬 Scene Director

A director's desk right under {{char}}'s last message — you call the shots, the AI plays them out.

- **🎬 Director** — 3 story-specific suggestions after every reply plus 16 commands (bad news, uninvited guest, secret revealed, confession, rival, time skip, flashback, cliffhanger…). Mix up to 3, choose the strength; clashing commands are caught before they reach the AI.
- **🧵 Story threads** — finds unresolved hooks in your roleplay; activate, develop, resolve or drop them.
- **🎲 Fate** — common, uncommon, rare or ✨ legendary twists; accept, save for later or reroll. Legendary takes are golden, never repeat and sometimes appear on their own.
- **🌙 Atmosphere** — 9 moods and dials for tension, romance, mystery, drama, humor and pacing.
- **🔀 What if…?** — 3 alternative directions for the very next reply.
- **📜 Chronicle** — every directed moment logged and linked to its message.
- **🛡️ {{user}} stays yours** — the director never decides your actions, words or feelings. One AI request per reply, a manual mode to save tokens, Tavo model or any OpenAI-compatible API.

## 📅 Event Calendar

Ever noticed how the model slacks on your schedule in a university or office roleplay? And holidays — it couldn't care less, until on your birthday your char suddenly pulls a gift out of thin air 😪 Event Calendar makes the AI **remember**: classes, work shifts, trainings, habits, daily routine, birthdays and holidays.

- **🕰 Story time, not real time** — follows the in-story date and time from the chat (works together with Phone and Reproductive System). A lecture from 10:00 to 12:00 doesn't end at 10:20 unless the story gives a reason; realistic, free and fixed-duration modes.
- **✨ Schedule from the roleplay** — reads the character, your persona and the chat, works out where you study or work (say, an architecture student) and proposes a weekly timetable with real subjects, times and rooms.
- **📅 Calendar** — month grid, day timeline with checklists, to-do list, event pages with lore; recurring events (weekdays, chosen days, monthly, yearly) with single-occurrence moves and cancellations.
- **🔔 The AI doesn't forget** — hidden reminders before events (a week … 5 minutes, for whom you choose), statuses (in progress, done, left early, missed, cancelled, moved), time skips reconciled, pop-up notifications in the chat.
- **🎊 Holidays & personal dates** — the closer the holiday, the more the world prepares; your char has thoughts about it and a gift that's prepared step by step; participants with their own plans and surprises; birthdays and anniversaries with "does the character know"; a recap the next day with consequences remembered.
- **📝 Diary, 🌙 dark theme, 🌐 RU / EN / 中** — the language button also switches the language of everything the plugin builds.

## 🧪 Prompt Debugger

See how many tokens every generation spends and what your prompt is made of: preset entries, character card, persona, triggered lorebook entries (constant + keyword), chat history and the model reply. Shows context-limit fill, block shares, cost at your prices, per-chat and per-session totals, and an **Estimate now** button to check the prompt size before you send. Numbers are estimates (±10–20%) — TavoAi does not expose the provider's exact token usage to plugins.

## 📱 Phone

A full iPhone inside your roleplay: lock screen with Face ID / passcode, Dynamic Island, notifications and smooth iOS-style animations. Text your character (photos, voice notes, reactions — they send selfies and text first when it fits the story), call each other, scroll Instagram, Twitter (X), Telegram channels, Discord, live streams and a dating app, answer anonymous questions and spread rumors in Whisper. Plus a bank with cards and Apple Pay, a shop with cart, bets on what happens next in the story with blackjack, roulette and a loan shark, music picked for the current scene, Google-style search, weather, notes, a gallery and games you can play against your character. Everything you do on the phone is quietly passed to the AI, and the phone's clock always follows the RP time.

## ⬇️ Install

1. Download the `.tpg` files from this repository (or the [**Releases**](https://github.com/floryhibi/Tavo_Plugins_by_floryhibi/releases/latest) page), or install from [**Tavo Hub**](https://hub.tavoai.dev/creators/HQ4G1).
2. TavoAi → **Plugins** → import each `.tpg`.
3. Open the plugin panel and adjust settings.
